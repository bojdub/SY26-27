#5 orange juice



Orange juice is a pretty good drink when done right. It is a bit sour but not to sour. It is also pretty healthy when freshly squeezed.
  • Orange Juice is the Official State Beverage of Florida

  • Orange juice is the most popular juice among US consumers.

  • Over half of all domestically produced orange juice comes from Florida oranges.
